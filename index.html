The model

One foundry. Many signatures.

Chefs know what is missing from a kitchen. A depth, a diameter, a handle, a colour. Cocreation turns that knowledge into a product, without the chef needing to own a factory.

I.

The chef designs

Dimensions, shape, lid, handles, enamel colour, branding. The product is built around how the chef cooks.

II.

We manufacture

La Cocotte Française produces the mould, casts, enamels and finishes every piece to its own standard.

III.

The chef sells

The finished piece carries the chef's brand. It is sold in the restaurant, online and to the chef's audience, at market price.

The foundation

Built on a proven range.

Every cocreation starts from La Cocotte Française's existing enamelled cast iron. The construction is proven. Only the details change.

The process

Two months, start to finish.

Four stages, one fixed calendar. Preorders open on day one, so the run can be partly sold before the first piece is cast.

  1. Suggestion

    The chef submits the idea: the base model, the changes, the intended use, the colours.

    Weeks 1–2

  2. Discussion & testing

    Our team checks whether it can be cast and is safe to use, and the chef tests prototypes and samples in their own kitchen.

    Weeks 2–3

  3. Design chosen

    Final dimensions, details, branding and the colour split across six batches are signed off.

    Week 4

  4. Mould & production

    The mould is produced and 300 units are cast. Enamel is applied in six batches of 50 in the chosen colours, then the pieces are finished, inspected and delivered.

    Weeks 5–8
