The model

One process. Many signatures.

Chefs know what they want in a kitchen. A depth, a diameter, a handle, a colour. Cocreation leverages that knowledge into a product.

I.

The chef proposes

Dimensions, shape, lid, handles, enamel colour. The product is built around how the chef cooks.

II.

The manufacturer produces

An established maker produces the mould, casts, enamels and finishes every piece to its own standard.

III.

The chef sells

The finished piece carries the Cocreation name. It is sold in the restaurant, online and to the chef's audience, at market price.

The starting point

Built on proven products.

In the first use case, each cocreation starts from La Cocotte Française's existing enamelled cast iron. The construction is proven. Only the details change.

The process

Open until the design is chosen. Two months after.

The road to a final design takes as long as it needs. Once the design is chosen, the calendar is fixed: two months of mould and production, with preorders open for the whole period.

  1. Suggestion

    The chef submits the idea: the base model, the changes, the intended use, the colours.

    Duration varies

  2. Discussion & testing

    The cocreation parties check that the design can be cast and is safe to use. The chef tests prototypes and samples in their own kitchen.

    Duration varies

  3. Design chosen

    Final dimensions, details and the colour split across six batches are signed off.

    Sign-off

  4. Mould & production

    The mould is produced and 300 units are cast. Enamel is applied in six batches of 50 in the chosen colours, then the pieces are finished, inspected and delivered. Preorders can be placed throughout.

    2 months
